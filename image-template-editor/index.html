One-time setup

  1. Export your template from Photopea: open your design, hide the sample photo and sample text layers (eye icons), then File → Export As → PNG.
  2. Load that PNG here with “Load template” →, or drop it on this canvas.
  3. Drag the photo frame over the spot where pictures go, and drag each text into place.

Everything is remembered in this browser. After setup, each new image is just: upload photo, type the name and district, Download PNG.
